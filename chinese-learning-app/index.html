About the Project

A personal Android app to learn Chinese vocabulary and characters. Built to make studying HSK vocabulary more engaging through flashcards, quizzes, and daily practice sessions.

The app includes multiple learning modes — flashcards for memorization, quizzes to test your knowledge, and a daily challenge to keep up a study streak.

Built entirely as a self-taught project to improve both my Chinese and my Android development skills.
