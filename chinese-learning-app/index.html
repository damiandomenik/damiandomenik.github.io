Overview

This is a Chinese learning app I built to help beginners pick up vocabulary and get ready for the HSK exams. It uses flashcards together with quizzes, so you learn the words and then check whether they actually stuck. It's a finished MVP, currently in closed beta.

Problem

Learning Chinese vocabulary takes consistent practice and repetition, and beginners need a simple, focused tool to build a strong foundation. But the market is the opposite of simple. It's crowded with learning apps that are packed with so many features they become overwhelming, and most of them push a subscription, an ad, or a long loading screen after just a few minutes of use. Instead of helping you learn, they get in the way.

Solution

I wanted the opposite: an app you can just open and start learning, with no sign-up, no clutter, and nothing forcing its way in front of the content. It sticks to the essentials. You learn vocabulary with flashcards and test yourself with quizzes, covering HSK 1 and HSK 2 to give beginners a clear starting point. The idea was to keep it minimal on purpose: do one thing well and stay out of the user's way.

Key Features

  • Chinese–English flashcards
  • HSK 1 and HSK 2 vocabulary sets
  • Interactive quizzes for knowledge assessment
  • No sign-up needed, just open the app and start
  • No ads, no paywall interrupting the learning
  • Simple, intuitive, beginner-friendly interface

My Role

I designed and developed the app end-to-end: the interface and navigation, the flashcard engine, the quiz logic, the local data structure, and the overall feel of it. It was a solo project, so all of that was mine to figure out, from how it looks down to how the data is stored.

Challenges

The hardest part was knowing what to leave out. It's tempting to keep adding features, but every extra one risks turning the app into exactly the kind of cluttered experience I was trying to avoid. So the real challenge was keeping it simple on purpose: enough to help people actually learn and retain vocabulary through repetition and testing, without burying them in options.

Results

The result is a fully functional MVP that covers the complete HSK 1 and HSK 2 vocabulary through flashcards and quizzes. It runs the full learning loop end to end, learn the words, then test them, and gives me a solid base to build more levels on top of later.

Future Improvements

I'm still working on it. The plan is to add more HSK levels and some more advanced content over time. The tricky part will be doing that without losing the simplicity that's the whole point, so anything new has to earn its place.
